Weezer's Top Five Best Albums

  1. Green Album

Weezer’s 2001 Green Album has to be their best. The band’s most famous song, “Island In The Sun,” comes from this album. “O Girlfriend” is what I consider to be one of Weezer’s best songs. I highly recommend this album!


      

     

  
        
      

        
  1. Pinkerton

        
Pinkerton from 1996 is another great Weezer album! The popular songs “I Just Threw Out The Love Of My Dreams” and “Pink Triangle” are featured on this album. “No Other One” is another awesome song from this album, and in my opinion, the best out of every song in this album.

      

      
      
 
      

      

      
      

        
      

        
  1. EWBAITE

        
EWBAITE (short for Everything Will Be Alright In The End) isn’t Weezer’s best album, but it’s definitely my favorite. The song “Foolish Father” really resonates with me. However, this 2014 album's best song has to be either “Lonely Girl” or “I’ve Had It Up To Here”.

      

      
      

      

      

      
      

       
      

        
  1. Red Album

        
 If you ask me, Weezer's Red Album from 2008 is incredibly underrated! From this album we receive “Pork And Beans,” another one of Weezer’s most famous songs. Two of my all time favorite songs belong to this album. Give the songs “King” and “Cold Dark World” a listen!

      

      
      

      

      

       
      

        
  1.  
    Blue Album

        
Everybody knows Weezer's Blue Album from 1994. Sure, it has some bangers, but because of its popularity, this album gets boring to listen to in my opinion. The song “The World Has Turned And Left Me Here” is my favorite from the Blue Album.
